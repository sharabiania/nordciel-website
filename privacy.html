Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who we are

Nordciel ("we", "us") is a software studio based in Ontario, Canada that publishes apps and games, including Pianotes. You can reach us at info@nordciel.com.

Information we collect

Our apps are designed to collect as little information as possible. Unless an app's store listing says otherwise:

  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • Settings and progress are stored on your device.
  • App stores (Google Play, Apple App Store, Roblox) may collect diagnostic and purchase data under their own privacy policies.

Third-party services

Where an app uses third-party services (for example analytics, crash reporting or ads), those services are listed in the app's store "Data safety" section and governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) you may ask what personal information we hold about you and request corrections or deletion by emailing info@nordciel.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
